# ConneCare-demo
AI-powered caregiving assistant prototype

**Reimagining Chronic Disease Caregiving with AI**

ConneCare is an AI-powered assistant designed to support caregivers of chronic disease patients in Hong Kong. The app helps reduce stress, manage daily caregiving tasks, and promote physical and mental well-being.

---

## Features

- **Calendar, Alarms & Reminders**  
  Track medical appointments, checkups, medication schedules, and dietary requirements.

- **Self-Monitoring & AI Guidance**  
  Record patient and caregiver health parameters like blood pressure, blood sugar, and body weight.

- **Mental & Physical Health Support**  
  Regular well-being check-ins, relaxation exercises, exercise reminders, and ergonomic guidance.

- **Community & Emergency Resources**  
  Access local support services, home assistance, temporary care options, and crisis hotlines.

- **Multilingual Support**  
  Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, and Indonesian with audio/text options.

- **Virtual Rewards & Collaboration**  
  Engagement dashboard and rewards system to encourage self-care and collaboration.
